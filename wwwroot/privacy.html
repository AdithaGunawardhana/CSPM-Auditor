Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Version 4.0

1. Information We Collect

Nexus CSPM collects only the minimum operational metadata necessary to deliver real-time cloud security scanning, audit reporting, and identity governance:

  • Account & Identity Data: When authenticating via Google Single Sign-On (Firebase Authentication), we retrieve your primary email address, display name, and avatar image URL to manage your session.
  • Identity Governance (IGA) Submissions: When applying for elevated administrator privileges, submitted details—including your full name, department, and operational access justification—are recorded in our dedicated cloud governance database (Firebase Firestore).
  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC) Uploads: Terraform (.tf) and Kubernetes (.yaml) manifests uploaded for static pre-flight analysis are parsed ephemerally in memory to identify security vulnerabilities.
  • Cloud Posture & Telemetry Findings: Audit metadata, rule evaluation outcomes across registered providers (AWS, Azure, GCP, Oracle, Kubernetes), and historical run statistics are logged locally.

2. How We Use Your Data

Collected telemetry and profile attributes are utilized strictly for core platform operations:

  • Multi-Tenant Data Isolation: Utilizing strict X-Tenant-ID header validations to ensure your infrastructure data, scan histories, and vulnerability reports are cryptographically sandboxed and completely inaccessible to other platform users.
  • Dynamic RBAC Authorization: Querying active Firestore records to verify whether your account holds an approved administrator role to unlock live SOAR remediation actions.
  • Governance Review Routing: Dispatching admin access request notices to the corporate governance portal (dxo-portal.html) and administrative notification channels.
  • AI Context Analysis: Passing resource rule codes and identifiers to our integrated AI analysis service to generate on-demand remediation guidance and threat explanations.
  • SOAR Execution: Initiating targeted automated patches (such as security group rule revocations or IAM policy tightening) at the direct command of an authorized administrator.

3. Architecture, Storage & Third-Party Services

Nexus operates on a hybrid architecture designed to maintain strict separation of concerns and data privacy:

Local Telemetry Engine

Cloud audit findings and scan histories are stored locally on the server instance via SQLite (cspm_audit.db) using Entity Framework Core. All database queries are strictly partitioned per-tenant.

Cloud Identity & Governance

Authentication sessions and admin access governance are hosted securely on Google Cloud infrastructure via Firebase Firestore.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or cloud telemetry to any third party.

4. Data Retention & Right to Erasure (Self-Service Deletion)

We respect full self-service data ownership. You have the right to review, update, or permanently erase your profile at any time:

By navigating to the User Profile page, users can trigger complete account deletion. This action automatically purges all linked access request records from the Firestore database and deletes the associated authentication identity.
